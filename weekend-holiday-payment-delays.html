Weekend and Holiday Payment Delays

Why a Friday evening payment takes until Tuesday, and what a three-day holiday weekend does to your money.

Home / Payment Delays

The Basic Problem

The Federal Reserve does not process payments on weekends or federal holidays. ACH settlements stop. Wire transfers stop. Check clearing stops. Your money sits in a holding pattern until the next business day opens.

This is not a bank being slow — it is the payment infrastructure itself pausing. No US bank can settle ACH transactions on a Saturday, regardless of how modern their technology is.

The Friday-Evening Gap

The most common delay scenario: you submit a payment after cutoff time on Friday. Here is the timeline:

Friday After Cutoff → Tuesday Arrival
  • Friday 5:00 PM — You submit after cutoff
  • Friday evening – Sunday — No processing (weekend)
  • Monday — First business day; transaction begins processing
  • Tuesday — Funds posted to recipient's account

Result: A payment submitted on Friday doesn't arrive until Tuesday — a 4-calendar-day wait for what felt like a same-week transaction.

The Holiday Weekend Gap

When a federal holiday falls on a Monday or Friday, the gap extends further:

ScenarioSubmit After CutoffFirst Business DayFunds ArriveCalendar Days
Normal weekendFriday PMMondayTuesday4
Monday holidayFriday PMTuesdayWednesday5
Friday holidayThursday PMMondayTuesday5
Thursday holiday + weekendWednesday PMFridayMonday5
Labor Day Weekend Example

Labor Day is the first Monday in September. You submit a payment at 4:00 PM on Friday after the cutoff:

  • Friday — Submitted after cutoff
  • Saturday – Sunday — Weekend, no processing
  • Monday (Labor Day) — Federal holiday, no processing
  • Tuesday — First business day, processing begins
  • Wednesday — Funds arrive

Result: A Friday-afternoon payment doesn't arrive until Wednesday — a 5-calendar-day wait.

Why It Feels Like the Money Disappeared

During the gap, the funds have left your account but have not appeared in the recipient's account. This is normal — the money is in transit between banks, waiting for the next settlement window. It has not been lost.

Your bank may show the transaction as "pending" or "processing." The recipient's bank has not yet received it. Once the Federal Reserve processes on the next business day, the funds move to the receiving bank and post to the account.

How to Avoid Weekend and Holiday Delays

  • Submit before Friday's cutoff — even better, submit by Thursday
  • Check the holiday calendar before scheduling time-sensitive payments
  • Use wire transfers for urgency — Fedwire settles same business day (but costs more and requires submission before the wire cutoff)
  • Plan around known holiday weekends — Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day all create 3-day weekends
