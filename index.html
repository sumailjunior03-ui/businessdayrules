Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Saturday a business day?
    No. In US banking and federal context, business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.
  • Do banks process payments on federal holidays?
    No. The Federal Reserve is closed on federal holidays, and ACH settlements do not occur on those days.
  • Why did my Friday payment not arrive until Tuesday?
    If you submitted after the bank's cutoff time on Friday, processing begins on the next business day (Monday). If Monday is a federal holiday, processing starts Tuesday.
